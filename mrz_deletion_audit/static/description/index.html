Deletion Audit Trail

Know who deleted which record, when, and what it contained.

A record disappears and nobody knows what happened? Standard Odoo keeps no trace of deleted records. Deletion Audit Trail keeps a permanent, read-only log of every deletion, with a full snapshot of the data, including the lines and child records that were deleted with it.

A deleted quotation: who deleted it, when, from where, and every field it contained.

Who, when, from where

User, date and time, IP address, browser, and channel: user interface, external API, website, scheduled action or script.

Full data snapshot

Every field of the deleted record, readable field by field: customer names, tags, amounts, dates and statuses, plus the list of its attachments.

Lines & child records

Invoice lines, order lines, stock moves... the records deleted together with the main record are captured too, and linked to it.

Captured before anything changes

The snapshot is taken before Odoo starts deleting, so you see the record as it really was, not a half-cancelled version.

Search deleted data

Find a deleted record by any value it contained: an email, a reference, an amount, a product name...

Tamper-proof

Nobody can edit or delete a log from the interface, not even administrators. Logs are only removed by your retention policy.

Using Odoo's Data Recycle?

Data Recycle deletes old records without keeping any trace of what they contained. With Deletion Audit Trail installed, every record it deletes is logged with its full data: as a Scheduled Action for automatic rules, or with the name of the user who clicked Validate.

How it works

  1. Install the app: the main business documents (contacts, products, quotations, invoices, transfers, projects, tasks, employees...) are tracked out of the box.
  2. Add or remove models in Deletion Audit > Configuration > Tracking Rules, or enable Track All Business Models in the settings.
  3. Open Deletion Audit > Deleted Records to see every deletion, group them by user, document type or date, and analyse them in pivot and graph views.

One rule per document type: track it or ignore it, capture its child records, and leave sensitive fields out of the snapshot.

Or track every business model at once, and decide how long the logs are kept.

Tracking rules

  • Track specific models, or all business models at once
  • Ignore the models you do not care about
  • Exclude sensitive fields from the snapshot
  • Turn the capture of child records on or off per model
  • Technical models are skipped automatically

Security & privacy

  • Auditors only see the logs of the models they are allowed to read
  • Administrators see all logs and manage the rules
  • Multi-company: logs follow the company of the deleted record
  • Passwords, tokens and secrets are never stored
  • Retention policy: purge logs automatically after N days

Reliable by design

  • The log is written in the same transaction as the deletion: if the deletion fails, no log is kept
  • Records archived instead of deleted are not logged
  • Bulk deletions of thousands of records are captured in batches
  • A capture problem never blocks the deletion itself

Deletions made together

  • See every record deleted in the same operation
  • Browse the tree of cascaded records
  • Know which field linked a child record to its parent
  • Know when a user acted through another user's session
